What the numbers mean

Decode and prefill are medians of the engine-reported rates. The first completion is a warm-up and is not counted.

Peak model RAM is shown when the engine reports a positive value. WASM heap is the reliable cross-model comparison.

Workers run one at a time and are terminated afterward, avoiding cross-model memory contamination.
